C64 Screen

Snapshot of the C64 text screen ($0400 + $D800 color RAM). Click Refresh for a fresh frame, or Play to poll continuously (4 Hz). Works for text-mode programs (BASIC, menus, GEOS-text); bitmap modes won't render correctly.

Note: each refresh briefly pauses the 6510 to DMA-read C64 RAM. Harmless for menus, BASIC, and most programs, but a vbl-tight demo or running disk operation may glitch slightly during Live polling. Use single Refresh if you need a non-intrusive look.

Data Streams

Start/stop UDP streams. Defaults are multicast (239.0.1.64–66) — receiver joins the group, no ARP/host lookup needed. Unicast IPs work too if the host is up on your LAN.

The video and audio streams use the U64 II's custom packet format; VLC and ffplay can't decode them directly — use Gideon's "U64 Stream Viewer" or a compatible decoder.

Note: starting a Video stream automatically stops Debug.
